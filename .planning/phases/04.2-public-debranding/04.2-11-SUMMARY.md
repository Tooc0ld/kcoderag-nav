## Decisions Made

- The workflow uses six explicit jobs rather than a synthetic static matrix receipt. This keeps the four platform job identities independently observable and prevents YAML presence from becoming evidence.
- The package job performs pack, audit, tar scan, packaged smoke and upload inside one Node process so the upload consumer receives the exact Plan 09 in-memory lease buffer, not a repacked or registry-fetched candidate.
- Downloaded raw artifact cleanup is allowed only for a strict child of the runner temporary directory; a broad or unrelated root is rejected before the lease is created.
- The final validator writes only an ephemeral workflow result. It does not update tracked readiness evidence or claim the four actual lanes passed.
