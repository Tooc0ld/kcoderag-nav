| canonical/Git/npm/tar bytes → parser/generator | untrusted paths, encodings, archive entries and output cross into trusted metadata |
| trusted metadata → package/host/evidence | a false or leaking verdict can become public assurance |

|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| T-04.2-06-02 | Denial of service | oversized/binary/blob/member input | high | mitigate | fixed archive/blob/member/count/path/output/time limits and fail-closed decoding |
| T-04.2-06-03 | Tampering | Unicode/case/separator bypass | high | mitigate | shared NFKC/lower/separator core with synthesized variants and negative controls |
